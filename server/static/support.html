Getting in touch

Write to natecodenew@gmail.com. Say your napsack username and what happened; never send your password or recovery key.

Common questions

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, choose "Forgot your password?" and use your recovery key: everything stays readable. Without one, an admin can give you a reset code, but messages from before the reset can't be opened again, since only your old password could unlock them.

How do I add a friend?

In Friends, show them your code to scan, send them your link, or type their username. Someone new to napsack signs up with your link and you're friends right away.

Someone's "safety code changed".

Their key changed, which normally only happens when their account was reset. Compare safety codes with them in person, or another way you trust, before you send anything.

I don't get notifications.

Check that notifications are on for napsack in your phone's settings, and that the chat isn't muted. napsack doesn't notify you while it's open on your screen.
